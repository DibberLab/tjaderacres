The rough is rough in summer months — come in fall or spring for the most enjoyable rounds. And there are no garbage cans on the course (bears!), so please carry out everything you carry in.

The Course

Tight, wooded, and worth the drive.

Jon Tjader bought this land in 2013 and spent two years cutting fairways before the first baskets went in. What opened in 2015 as 18 holes grew to 20, then 21, and finally the full 27 — a dedicated disc golf property, built by a disc golfer, carved hole by hole out of the northwoods.

Wooded & hilly terrain

Tight but fair fairways through mature forest with real elevation change. Most holes run 228–432 ft. Moderate-to-hard difficulty — bring your whole bag.

Concrete tees, Prodigy T1 baskets

Solid concrete tee pads on every hole and Prodigy T1 targets throughout. Cart-friendly and dog-friendly, with restrooms at the parking lot and hole C. No garbage cans (bears) — pack it out.

Plan 3 to 3.5 hours

The full 27 runs about 3.2 miles of golf with lots of elevation change (not recommended for limited mobility). No drinking water on the course — bring your own.

Nine layouts, three loops

A Loop · 9 holesThe opener — a fair warm-up that still makes you shape shots.
B Loop · 9 holesDeeper into the woods, tighter windows, bigger elevation.
C Loop · 9 holesThe back nine — mix and match loops for 9, 18, or the full 27.

Hole-by-hole maps, distances, and scorecards for all 9 layout combinations live on UDisc — the course is ranked among the top 100 in the United States.

Also On The Property

Tjiny Acres — the 18-hole putter course

Opened in 2023 right next to the big course, Tjiny Acres packs 18 par-3 putter holes into one very hilly mile. Holes average around 100 feet (91–138 ft), marked by flags with Mach III baskets — short, but reviewers call it highly technical. Perfect warm-up, cool-down, or a full round with the kids.
